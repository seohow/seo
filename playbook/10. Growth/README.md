# 10. Growth

> The compounding-growth category. Once the operational basics are in place across categories 1-9, sustained ranking and traffic growth comes from four levers: building topical authority, scaling content production, acquiring links systematically, and experimenting your way to what works. The discipline of moving from a working SEO program to a compounding one.

## What this category covers

Growth is what happens after the basics are in place. Strategy (Cat 1) sets the direction; On-page (2), Technical (3), Content (4) ship the work; Analytics (5) measures it; Off-page (6), UX (7), International (8), and AI SEO (9) deepen specific surfaces. Once those layers are operating at baseline competence, the question shifts from "are we doing SEO correctly" to "are we compounding." That's what Growth covers.

The four levers in this category are the highest-leverage compounding mechanisms. Topical authority compounds because deep coverage on a topic earns the brand citations and rankings on adjacent topics for free — once you're recognised as the authority on mineral sunscreen, you start ranking for tinted SPF, vitamin C + SPF interaction, and dozens of cluster-adjacent queries you didn't directly target. Content scaling compounds because production discipline plus consistent quality lets you cover more of the cluster surface area, which feeds topical authority, which feeds rankings. Link acquisition compounds because high-authority links earned this quarter accelerate rankings for content shipped next quarter. And SEO experiments compound because every test produces a learning that improves the next test, the next brief, the next investment.

For Field & Sun, the question this category answers is: "we have the basics in place — now how do we go from 8,400 organic sessions/month to 17,000 in six months, and 50,000+ in 18-24 months, without burning out the team or compromising brand voice?" The answer isn't more of the same. It's strategic posture changes (topical authority), operational system upgrades (content scaling), and learning-velocity improvements (experiments). This category provides the frameworks for those decisions.

## How the sub-topics fit together

```
Strategic posture          Operational system        Acquisition system        Learning system
─────────────────          ──────────────────        ──────────────────        ───────────────
01. Topical Authority      02. Content Scaling       03. Link Acquisition      04. SEO Experiments
(what you're known for)    (how you ship)            (how you earn authority)  (how you learn)
```

1. **[01. Topical Authority](01.%20Topical%20Authority.md)** — the multi-year strategic posture. Distinct from Pillar Pages (Content SEO leaf 02), which is the artifact-level technique. Topical authority is the compounding outcome that comes from sustained pillar-and-cluster coverage, earned mentions, entity-graph alignment, and topic-specific link acquisition over years. This is the moat once it's built.
2. **[02. Content Scaling](02.%20Content%20Scaling.md)** — the operational discipline of moving production volume from "what one writer can produce" to "what a content system can produce" without losing quality. Distinct from Content Briefing (the artifact) and Programmatic SEO (the technique for scaling unique content via templates). This is the people / process / quality system that makes higher volume sustainable.
3. **[03. Link Acquisition](03.%20Link%20Acquisition.md)** — strategic, programmatic, and asset-driven link acquisition. Distinct from Link Building (Off-page leaf 01), which is the tactical per-prospect outreach surface. Link Acquisition asks the upstream question: which assets become link magnets in the first place? How do you systematise creation and promotion of link-worthy content?
4. **[04. SEO Experiments](04.%20SEO%20Experiments.md)** — structured SEO experimentation. Distinct from UX A/B Testing (UX leaf 04), which is conversion-focused. SEO Experiments are about learning what moves rankings, citation rate, and feature presence — through controlled tests on titles, internal linking, schema, content patterns, and freshness signals. Statistical rigor + SEO-safety + longitudinal tracking.

## A suggested workflow

If you're working through this category for the first time, the highest-leverage sequence is:

**Pass 1 — Strategic baseline:**

1. Run `assess-topical-authority` on your 2-3 most-strategic clusters to baseline current authority and identify the gap to topical-authority status.
2. Run `plan-content-scaling-operation` to baseline current production capacity and identify the system upgrades needed for the next 6-12 months.
3. Run `plan-link-magnet-strategy` to identify which assets in your current and planned content portfolio are link-magnet candidates.

**Pass 2 — Operational upgrades:**

1. Implement the topical-authority gap-fill content (cluster spokes, supporting evergreen, refresh of weak pages).
2. Ship the content-scaling system upgrades (brief template improvements, freelancer / agency onboarding, editorial workflow, QA pass).
3. Produce 1-2 link-magnet assets per quarter (original research, primary-evidence content, calculators, tools, definitive guides).

**Pass 3 — Learning loop:**

1. Run `design-seo-experiment` on one structured test per quarter — title rewrite at scale, internal-link change, schema A/B, content pattern test.
2. Capture learnings; feed back into briefs / standards / playbook.

**Pass 4 — Compound:**

1. Quarterly re-baseline of topical authority, scaling capacity, link velocity, experiment learnings.
2. Each quarter's investment is informed by the previous quarter's measurement.

## Common pitfalls

- **Treating Growth as "do more of what we're doing."** Growth is a posture change, not a volume change. More posts at the same quality bar produces flat returns; better systems for production + acquisition + learning produces compounding returns.
- **Topical authority without sustained investment.** Building topical authority on a cluster takes 6-18 months of sustained pillar + cluster + supporting + earned-mention + link investment. Quarterly hops between topics produces no authority anywhere.
- **Content scaling that breaks brand voice.** Scaling production via cheap freelancers without editorial discipline produces homogenous content that ranks poorly and damages brand. Scale the system (brief template, editorial review, QA), not just the writer count.
- **Link acquisition without link-magnet assets.** Outreach without a genuinely link-worthy asset is a slog. Invest in the asset first; outreach second.
- **Experiments without statistical rigor.** "We changed the title and ranking went up" is not an experiment — it's anecdote. Pre-register the hypothesis, sample size, success criteria; track for the duration; accept null results.
- **Experiments without SEO safety.** A test that crashes ranking on the test pages is not safe to run. UX leaf 04 covers the SEO-safety review for A/B tests; the same discipline applies here.
- **Skipping measurement.** Growth is a longitudinal game. Without quarterly re-baselines (topical authority, scaling capacity, link velocity, experiment learnings), the brand can't tell if it's compounding or running in place.
- **Confusing volume with growth.** 50 posts/month with low quality and no link strategy is busy work, not growth. The compounding lever is the quality of the system, not the count of the outputs.
- **Treating links as a separate game from content.** Link acquisition flows from link-magnet content, which flows from topical authority, which flows from cluster strategy. The categories are tightly coupled; siloing them produces underperformance everywhere.
- **Under-investing in the learning loop.** Most SEO programs run zero structured experiments per quarter. Two well-designed experiments per year out-learns ten years of "let's see if it works" iteration.

## Where to go after this

Once Growth is operating:

- **Re-run earlier categories with Growth-informed priorities.** Topical-authority gaps re-prioritise cluster mapping (Strategy + Content SEO); scaling-system upgrades re-shape briefing (Content SEO leaf 08); link-magnet assets re-shape Digital PR (Off-page leaf 03).
- **Quarterly cadence.** Each Growth skill is quarterly. The cadence is the discipline.
- **Cross-reference everything.** Growth is the integrating category — Topical Authority touches Content / Off-page / AI SEO; Content Scaling touches Content / Strategy; Link Acquisition touches Off-page / Content / AI SEO; Experiments touch every category. Treat the cross-links as load-bearing.
- **Revisit parked categories.** If the brand reaches a maturity stage where the parked categories (Local SEO, Video / YouTube SEO, Newsletter, Podcast, dedicated E-commerce SEO) become relevant, revisit them as new categories (see AGENTS.md "Future categories under consideration").
