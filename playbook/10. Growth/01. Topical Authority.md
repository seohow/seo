# Topical Authority

> The strategic posture of being recognised — by Google, by AI search engines, by readers, by the press — as an authoritative source on a topic. The compounding moat that makes everything else easier once it's built. Multi-year investment; no shortcuts.

## What it is

Topical authority is the position your brand holds in Google's (and increasingly AI systems') topical map. It's the answer to "when someone searches for anything in this topic area, do they find us, expect us, and trust us?" Topical authority isn't a single artifact — it's the accumulated effect of sustained pillar-and-cluster coverage, earned mentions across the web, entity-graph alignment (Wikidata, schema, knowledge panel), citation share in AI Overviews and Perplexity / ChatGPT / Claude answers, and topic-specific link acquisition over years. Once built, it compounds: pages rank with less effort, new content earns rankings on adjacent topics for free, link prospects respond to outreach without a hard sell, and AI systems cite the brand reflexively.

The crucial distinction from related topics: **Pillar Pages** (Content SEO leaf 02) is the artifact-level technique — produce a comprehensive pillar page on the topic, link spokes to it. **Topic Clusters** (Content SEO leaf 03) is the architectural pattern — pillar + spokes + internal-linking. **Topical Authority** (this leaf) is the *compounding outcome* of doing the artifact and architecture work consistently for 12-24+ months on a chosen topic. It's the strategic posture, not the artifact.

For Field & Sun, topical authority is the answer to the question "where can we be — over the next three years — the brand that gets cited reflexively?" The honest answer is *not* "all of skincare" (Wirecutter, Strategist, Healthline territory) and *not* "all of mineral sunscreen" (publisher review territory plus established brands). The defensible answer is something narrower: "tinted mineral sunscreen for darker skin tones with ingredient-led depth and original Fitzpatrick I-VI evidence." That's the topic on which Field & Sun can plausibly become the recognised authority over 18-24 months. Once it's built, the brand earns rankings on adjacent ingredient and SPF queries, AI Overview citations expand from one query to dozens, link prospects respond to outreach because the brand is now a known entity in the topic, and the competitive moat is real.

## Why it matters

Three reasons. First, **topical authority compounds**. Pages on a topic where the brand has authority rank with less work than pages on a topic where the brand is unknown. New content launches in the topic area earn rankings within weeks, not months. AI systems cite the brand on adjacent queries unprompted. Each year's investment makes the next year's investment more productive — the opposite of the diminishing-returns dynamic on operational SEO work.

Second, **topical authority is increasingly load-bearing for AI search**. AI Overviews and conversational AI cite a small set of authority-recognised sources per query. Brands without topical authority lose visibility regardless of where they rank in blue links. The shift to AI-mediated search makes the authority game even more concentrated than the traditional SEO game.

Third, **topical authority is defensive**. Once a brand has it on a topic, competitors trying to displace it face a 12-24-month investment. Most won't make it. The brand that gets there first on a defensible topic holds the position for years. Defensibility justifies the upfront investment.

## Core concepts

- **Coverage breadth.** The number of distinct queries / topics within the cluster the brand has substantive content for. Authority requires breadth — a single excellent pillar plus 3 spokes is not authority; a pillar plus 25 cluster spokes plus refresh content plus original research is.
- **Coverage depth.** The depth per piece. Authority content is depth-rich (3,000+ words for pillars; original evidence; comparative tables; named source citations) rather than thin (500-word listicle). Depth-poor coverage at high breadth is recognisable spam patterns; depth-rich coverage at high breadth is authority.
- **Earned mentions and links.** Authority is a function of who else thinks you're an authority. Earned mentions in publications, links from authoritative sites, citations in AI Overviews, brand mentions in social and forum content all reinforce the signal. Without external endorsement, internal coverage doesn't translate to authority.
- **Entity-graph alignment.** Wikidata entry, Wikipedia presence (where notable), schema `sameAs` linking the brand entity to the wider knowledge graph, consistent entity descriptions across the web. Authority brands have aligned entity-graph signals; non-authority brands don't.
- **Topic-specific link velocity.** New links acquired this quarter from sources in the topic area. A skincare-authority brand earns new skincare links every quarter; a brand acquiring random / off-topic links is not building topical authority even if absolute link counts grow.
- **AI citation share.** What % of priority topic queries cite the brand in major AI search engines. Authority brands have rising citation share over time on the topic; non-authority brands have flat-to-falling share even if absolute coverage grows.
- **Brand-query volume.** Direct branded search volume in the topic area ("Field & Sun mineral sunscreen review" / "Field & Sun vs Supergoop"). Authority brands grow brand-query volume in their topic area; pure-SEO brands without authority have flat brand-query growth.
- **Time horizon.** Topical authority is a 12-24+ month investment. Quarterly hops between topics — start a vitamin C cluster, abandon for retinol, abandon for sunscreen — produces no authority anywhere. Stay on the topic until you've earned the position.
- **Topical-authority graveyards.** Topics dominated by Wikipedia (generic ingredient explainers); by Wirecutter / Strategist / Byrdie (commercial review queries on broad categories); by Healthline / Verywell (medical / dermatology generic content). Pick topics outside these graveyards.
- **Defensibility test.** Before investing 18-24 months in a topical-authority play, can you name what makes the brand defensibly the authority? "We make good products" is not defensibility. "We have the largest Fitzpatrick I-VI photographic library in the SPF industry plus founder dermatology adjacency" is defensibility.

## A worked example

> **Scenario:** Field & Sun is at 8,400 organic sessions/month in 2026-Q2. Strategy goal: 50,000+ sessions/month in 18-24 months. The brand has cluster maps and is shipping content, but ranking gains are slow and AI citation rate is low.
>
> **Step 1.** Run `assess-topical-authority` on the brand's three priority clusters: mineral sunscreen, vitamin C, refills.
>
> - **Mineral sunscreen cluster:** coverage 6 of 25 priority queries; depth thin on 4 of 6; earned-link velocity ~1 link/quarter; AI citation share 8%; brand-query volume flat; entity-graph signals weak.
> - **Vitamin C cluster:** coverage 5 of 22 priority queries; depth thin throughout; competitor dominance from Wirecutter / Strategist + Healthline / Verywell; AI citation share 0%; defensibility: weak (no original research; no founder credentials specific to vitamin C).
> - **Refills cluster:** coverage 4 of 8 priority queries; depth medium; competitor dominance limited (sustainability D2C is less crowded); AI citation share 50% (already strong); brand-query volume rising; defensibility: real (refill pouches genuinely work; testing data + customer cohort exists).
>
> **Step 2.** Strategic call:
>
> - **Mineral sunscreen:** invest in topical authority — defensibility is real per generative-SEO Pick 1 (Fitzpatrick I-VI photography moat). 18-month plan: pillar + 25 spokes + 4 link-magnet assets + 3 original-research pieces + sustained Off-page work + AI citation tracking.
> - **Vitamin C:** *do not* invest in topical authority — defensibility is weak; topical-authority graveyard via publisher dominance. Continue tactical content but de-prioritise.
> - **Refills:** invest in topical authority — already on a strong trajectory; the brand can be the recognised refillable-skincare authority within 12 months given the smaller competitive set. 12-month plan: pillar refresh + 8 supporting pieces + 2 original-research pieces (refill efficacy / waste data) + Wikidata + sustained Off-page in sustainability publications.
>
> **Step 3.** Multi-quarter plan baked into the editorial calendar (Content SEO), backlog (Strategy), link-magnet plan (Growth leaf 03), and experiment plan (Growth leaf 04). Cross-cluster sequencing: mineral sunscreen Pick 1 ships Q2; refills pillar refresh + 2 spokes ship Q2-Q3; sustained cluster fill across both Q3-Q4-Q1.
>
> **Step 4.** Re-baseline at end of Q4 (90 days post Pick 1, mid-flight on refills):
>
> - **Mineral sunscreen:** coverage 12 of 25; AI citation share 32%; brand-query volume +180%; earned-link velocity 4 links / quarter; entity-graph improvements (Wikidata pending).
> - **Refills:** coverage 7 of 8; AI citation share 70%; brand-query volume +40% (already smaller base); earned-link velocity 6 links / quarter (sustainability publications responsive).
> - **Vitamin C:** unchanged (correctly de-prioritised).
>
> **Step 5.** End of year — Field & Sun is recognised within the industry as the authority on tinted mineral sunscreen for darker skin tones (mineral cluster) and on refillable skincare (refills cluster). Adjacent rankings start arriving without direct targeting; AI citation rate compounds; brand-query volume becomes a meaningful traffic source. Year 2 plan: deepen both, add a third cluster (TBD).

## How to do it

1. **Audit before you invest.** Run `assess-topical-authority` on 2-3 priority clusters. The audit catches authority graveyards (skip them) and identifies real defensibility (invest there).
2. **Pick clusters defensively.** Authority is a topic-by-topic decision. Pick clusters where (a) defensibility is real, (b) the competitor set is not Wikipedia / Wirecutter / Healthline territory, (c) the brand can sustain 12-24 months of investment, and (d) the topic matters commercially.
3. **Concentrate, don't spread.** 2 deep authority investments beat 5 shallow ones. The compounding lever is depth × time, not breadth × time.
4. **Plan multi-quarter, not quarterly.** Authority work spans 4-8 quarters of sustained investment. The plan is a roadmap, not a quarterly OKR.
5. **Bake authority work across categories.** Topical authority touches Content SEO (pillar / spokes / refresh), Off-page (topic-specific link acquisition), AI SEO (citation strategy), Strategy (cluster mapping), and Growth (this leaf orchestrates). Each category's work in the priority cluster compounds the others.
6. **Earn external signals deliberately.** Authority is partly external. Plan link-magnet assets per cluster (Growth leaf 03), pitch industry publications (Off-page leaf 03), submit to relevant Wikidata / Wikipedia entries (NLP Optimization), seek expert collaborations.
7. **Track quarterly, decide annually.** Use the audit's metrics (coverage, depth, link velocity, citation share, brand-query volume, entity-graph signals) as quarterly KPIs. Make the annual call (continue / pivot / harvest) based on multi-quarter direction.
8. **Resist quarterly hops.** When the topic gets boring, when ranking gains slow, when leadership wonders why we're still on this — stay the course. Authority is built on sustained investment; the temptation to pivot is the failure mode.
9. **Document the bet.** Write the topical-authority decision into per-business CLAUDE.md (decisions log) so the next agent / team / quarter doesn't accidentally pivot away.
10. **Plan the harvest.** Once authority is built, investment shifts from breadth (more spokes) to depth (refresh + original research) and adjacency (extending into related clusters from the authority position). Authority is the asset; harvest is the operating mode.

## Common pitfalls

- **Choosing topics in authority graveyards.** Generic vitamin C / generic mineral sunscreen / general skincare-tip queries are graveyards. The authorities are already there (Wikipedia / Wirecutter / Healthline). Pick narrower, defensibly-yours topics.
- **Quarterly topic-hopping.** Authority requires sustained investment. Hopping between topics every quarter produces no authority on any of them.
- **Volume without depth.** 50 thin posts on a cluster do not build authority; they signal spam patterns. 25 deep posts on a cluster build authority.
- **Coverage without external signals.** Internal coverage compounds only when paired with earned links / mentions / citations. Pure on-page work plateaus.
- **Authority play without defensibility.** "We're going to be the authority on mineral sunscreen" without a moat (original research, primary evidence, founder expertise) is wishful thinking against incumbents who already have the position.
- **Treating authority as a single-category problem.** Authority is cross-category — Content + Off-page + AI SEO + Strategy + Growth all contribute. Siloing the work to Content alone underperforms.
- **No measurement.** Without quarterly KPIs (coverage, depth, link velocity, citation share, brand-query volume, entity-graph signals), authority work is unaccountable and pivot-prone.
- **Over-investment without harvest.** Once authority is built, continuing to expand breadth indefinitely produces diminishing returns. Harvest the authority — refresh + adjacency + commercial conversion — at the right moment.
- **Building authority in commercially-uninteresting topics.** Authority on a topic with no commercial relevance is vanity. Pick topics that are both defensibly-yours and commercially-meaningful for the brand.
- **Pivoting on short-term ranking dips.** Authority work has 6-18-month feedback loops. Quarterly ranking dips are noise; the multi-quarter trajectory is signal.
