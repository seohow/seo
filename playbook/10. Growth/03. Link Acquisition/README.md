# Link Acquisition

> The strategic, asset-driven, programmatic side of earning authority links. Distinct from per-prospect outreach (Off-page leaf 01) — this leaf asks the upstream question: which assets become link magnets in the first place, and how do you systematically produce and promote them?

## What it is

Link acquisition is the strategic discipline of producing assets that are inherently link-worthy and promoting them at scale into the right link graph. The leaf operates upstream of tactical link building: tactical link building executes outreach against existing assets; link acquisition decides which assets to build, what makes them link magnets, how to produce them on a quarterly cadence, and how to integrate them with topical-authority and AI-citation strategy.

The crucial distinction: **Link Building** (Off-page leaf 01) is the tactical surface — outreach manual, prospect lists, email templates, pitch sequences, link-velocity tracking. **Digital PR** (Off-page leaf 03) is the publication-led acquisition surface — press pitches, journalist outreach, HARO. **Link Acquisition** (this leaf) is the strategic / programmatic / asset-design layer that feeds both — what original research are we publishing this quarter; what definitive guides, free tools, calculators, original data, primary-evidence assets are we creating; how do we make sure the assets become natural link targets rather than hopeful pitches.

For Field & Sun, link acquisition is the question: "in 18 months we want to be the recognised authority on tinted mineral sunscreen for darker skin tones — what 4-6 link-magnet assets do we ship between now and then to ensure publishers, dermatology blogs, and trade press cite us reflexively?" The answer isn't "send more pitches." It's a deliberate programme: original Fitzpatrick I-VI research, a definitive guide that every other publication wants to reference, a free SPF-comparison calculator, a primary-research piece on iron oxide tinting, and high-evidence customer documentation. Each asset, well-executed, earns 30-100 links over its lifetime. Cumulative effect over 18 months: hundreds of cluster-relevant links from authority sources, which feeds topical authority (Growth leaf 01), which compounds.

## Why it matters

Three reasons. First, **outreach without link-magnet assets is a slog**. Pitching a generic blog post to publications produces low response rates and low link conversion. Pitching original research, primary-evidence content, or a uniquely useful tool produces high response rates and durable links. The asset is the leverage; outreach without it is the failure mode.

Second, **link-magnet assets compound across categories**. The same original-research piece that earns 50 backlinks also earns AI Overview citations (AI SEO leaf 03), feeds topical authority (Growth leaf 01), provides talking points for Digital PR (Off-page leaf 03), and lifts brand-query volume. The investment is one piece of work; the return is across categories.

Third, **link-magnet content is brand-distinctive**. Other brands can copy your tactical outreach; they can't replicate your original research or proprietary data. Asset-driven link acquisition builds defensibility that tactical outreach cannot.

## Core concepts

- **Link-magnet asset taxonomy.** Six recurring asset types that consistently earn links:
  - **Original research / primary data.** Surveys, customer testing, photographic libraries, longitudinal datasets. Hardest to produce; highest link-magnet value.
  - **Definitive guides.** Comprehensive, evidence-rich, source-cited deep dives that become the canonical reference on a topic.
  - **Free tools / calculators.** Useful, single-purpose utilities (SPF cost-per-day calculator; ingredient interaction checker). Linkable indefinitely.
  - **Comparative resources.** Side-by-side product / ingredient / methodology comparisons with consistent methodology and evidence.
  - **Visual / data assets.** Infographics, photographic libraries, datasets that publishers cite. Visual link-magnets often earn image-pack visibility too.
  - **Curated lists / databases.** "Every brand using iron oxide in mineral SPF" / "all FDA-approved sunscreen actives." Links flow from people referencing the list.
- **Asset-side leverage vs outreach-side leverage.** Asset-side: a great asset gets cited 50-200 times over its lifetime. Outreach-side: a great pitch gets a single link. Investment in asset quality compounds; investment in outreach scale doesn't.
- **Defensibility of the asset.** A link-magnet asset is durable in proportion to its replicability. Original research with proprietary data: very durable. Generic explainer with strong content: moderately durable. Listicle: not durable. Pick the asset shape with the most proprietary input.
- **Promotion is part of the asset.** A perfect asset that ships without promotion earns 5 links. The same asset with structured promotion earns 50. Plan promotion as part of the asset spec, not as an afterthought.
- **Refresh cycle.** Link-magnet assets decay. Original research from three years ago is less linkable than fresh. Refresh cadence (annual / biennial) keeps the asset live; without refresh, link velocity declines.
- **Cluster fit.** Assets are most effective when they support an active topical-authority play. A great asset on an unrelated topic produces links but doesn't compound the cluster strategy.
- **Asset velocity.** 4-6 link-magnet assets per year is typical for a D2C brand at scale. Below that, link velocity is bottlenecked; above that, asset quality usually drops.
- **Earned media path.** The path from asset to link runs through: Off-page outreach (publications, journalists, HARO); industry / trade press (cluster-specific publications); influencer / expert citations; organic discovery (search rank for the asset's target queries).
- **Anti-patterns.** Paid link schemes, PBNs, link exchanges, large-scale guest posting, comment / forum link-stuffing. These produce short-term link velocity at long-term penalty risk. This leaf does not advocate them.
- **Measurement.** Per-asset link tracking (Ahrefs / Semrush backlink monitor); per-asset citation tracking in AI search (cross-reference AI SEO leaf 05); per-asset organic traffic; per-asset secondary brand mentions.

## A worked example

> **Scenario:** Field & Sun has the topical-authority assessment (Growth leaf 01) recommending an 18-month investment on the mineral sunscreen cluster. Production capacity is being scaled (Growth leaf 02). Off-page outreach manual exists but needs link-magnet assets to feed it.
>
> **Step 1.** Run `plan-link-magnet-strategy` for the mineral sunscreen cluster + the refills cluster. Findings:
>
> - **Mineral sunscreen cluster — link-magnet candidates:**
>   1. **Original Fitzpatrick I-VI photographic study.** 200+ photos across 8 customers × 3 product applications under consistent lighting. Primary research; high-defensibility moat. 6-week production. Promotion to dermatology publications, beauty press, sustainability publications. Link target: 60-100 links over 24 months.
>   2. **Definitive iron oxide tinting guide.** Mechanism + chemistry + formulation considerations + visual evidence. 4-week production. Cross-references peer-reviewed studies. Link target: 30-50 links over 18 months.
>   3. **Free SPF cost-per-day calculator.** Single-purpose tool; users input bottle size + daily application rate; outputs cost-per-day and refill cadence. 3-week production (writer + dev). Refillable angle ties to brand. Link target: 50-80 links over 24 months (high lifetime value due to utility).
>   4. **Mineral SPF formulation database.** Curated database of ~40 mineral SPF products with active concentrations, tint range, white-cast outcome on Fitzpatrick I-VI, FDA approval status. 8-week production (research-heavy). Annual refresh. Link target: 80-120 links over 30 months.
> - **Refills cluster — link-magnet candidates:**
>   5. **Primary research on refill efficacy and waste reduction.** Original data: refill use frequency, packaging waste reduction, customer behaviour patterns. 6-week production (depends on customer-survey instrument). Sustainability publications target. Link target: 40-70 links over 24 months.
>   6. **Definitive refillable beauty buyers' guide.** Comprehensive cross-brand guide to refillable beauty (skincare + body + cosmetics). Methodology + evaluation framework + per-brand assessment. 6-week production. Link target: 50-80 links over 24 months.
> - **Total programme:** 6 assets over ~12 months production calendar; total link target 310-500 links over the assets' lifetimes; cluster authority compounding.
>
> **Step 2.** Sequencing:
>
> - **Q3 2026:** Asset 1 (Fitzpatrick photographic study) — already on the generative-SEO Pick 1 docket; doubles as link magnet. Asset 5 (refill primary research) parallel.
> - **Q4 2026:** Asset 2 (iron oxide tinting guide). Asset 6 (refillable beauty buyers' guide).
> - **Q1 2027:** Asset 3 (SPF cost-per-day calculator).
> - **Q2 2027:** Asset 4 (formulation database).
>
> **Step 3.** Per-asset spec: each asset gets a one-page brief with production team, timeline, defensibility argument, target publications, promotion plan (cross-reference Off-page leaf 05 outreach manual), measurement plan.
>
> **Step 4.** Promotion infrastructure: standardise the asset-launch promotion playbook — pre-launch outreach to 3-5 anchor publications; launch-day social + newsletter + email; post-launch outreach over 60 days; refresh schedule annual.
>
> **Step 5.** Measurement: per-asset link tracker; quarterly link-velocity by cluster; cross-reference with `audit-llm-visibility` to track citation rate lift on cluster queries.
>
> **Step 6.** Result over 18 months: 6 assets shipped; ~250 backlinks acquired (vs ~25 from previous-12-month outreach without assets); cluster topical authority lift visible; AI citation share lift on both clusters; brand-query volume rising.

## How to do it

1. **Tie link acquisition to topical authority.** Don't produce link-magnet assets on random topics. Tie to the priority cluster(s) from `assess-topical-authority` (Growth leaf 01).
2. **Pick asset types by defensibility.** Original research / primary data > free tools > definitive guides > comparative resources > visual / data assets > curated lists. Defensibility ladder; pick high.
3. **Run `plan-link-magnet-strategy` quarterly.** Output is a 4-6 asset programme over 12-24 months, sequenced by production cost + cluster priority.
4. **Specify each asset.** One-page brief: defensibility argument, target queries it answers, production team, timeline, promotion plan, link target, measurement plan.
5. **Allocate dedicated production capacity.** Asset production is *not* just content production. Original research + primary evidence + tool development requires research / photography / dev capacity. Cross-reference Growth leaf 02 scaling plan to ensure capacity exists.
6. **Build promotion into the asset.** Promotion plan locks before production starts. Anchor publications identified pre-launch; outreach scheduled at launch; refresh schedule defined.
7. **Refresh the assets annually.** Original research goes stale; calculators need data updates; databases need refreshing. Plan refresh cadence per asset.
8. **Avoid anti-patterns.** No paid link schemes, PBNs, link exchanges, scaled guest posting, or comment-stuffing. These produce short-term velocity at long-term penalty risk.
9. **Cross-reference with Digital PR.** Off-page leaf 03 (Digital PR) is the publication-pitching surface. Link-magnet assets are what Digital PR pitches. The leaves are tightly coupled.
10. **Measure quarterly.** Per-asset link counts; per-cluster link velocity; AI citation rate lift; brand-query volume. Adjust the programme based on data — assets that under-perform inform the next quarter's selection.

## Common pitfalls

- **Outreach without link-magnet assets.** Pitching generic blog posts produces low conversion. Invest in the asset first.
- **Link-magnet assets without promotion.** Great assets that ship without structured promotion earn 10% of their potential.
- **Random-topic asset programmes.** Assets that don't tie to a topical-authority play earn links without compounding.
- **Listicle as link-magnet.** Generic listicles produce some links but no defensibility. Pick higher up the defensibility ladder.
- **Single-asset thinking.** One link-magnet asset doesn't build authority. A programme of 4-6 over 12-24 months does.
- **Production cost under-budgeted.** Original research and primary evidence are expensive (photography, surveys, dev time, research time). Budget realistically.
- **Refresh neglect.** Assets shipped and never updated decay. Cadence-driven refresh keeps link velocity flowing.
- **Anti-pattern adoption under pressure.** When organic asset-driven acquisition is slow, the temptation to buy links / participate in PBNs spikes. Resist; the long-term penalty risk is real.
- **No measurement.** Without per-asset link tracking, the programme can't be optimised.
- **Bundling with content scaling.** Link-magnet asset production is *separate* capacity from cluster-fill content production. Scaling plan (Growth leaf 02) should allocate both explicitly.
- **Forgetting AI citation as a link-acquisition surface.** AI Overview / Perplexity / ChatGPT search citation tends to follow the same content shape as link-magnet content. The investment compounds across both surfaces.
