# SERP Features

> The on-SERP real estate that increasingly answers queries before a click happens — featured snippets, People Also Ask, AI Overviews, knowledge panels, image and video carousels, shopping packs. Earning these features captures visibility regardless of where you sit in the blue-link rankings.

*Last reviewed: 2026-07*

## What it is

A SERP feature is any element on a search results page that isn't a standard blue link with title + URL + description. The category covers familiar features (featured snippets, People Also Ask, image packs, video carousels, knowledge panels, shopping packs, local packs) and newer AI-driven ones (AI Overviews, AI-generated PAA expansions, generative shopping summaries). For most commercial and informational queries in 2026, SERP features now occupy more vertical space than the first three blue-link results combined — meaning the ranking-only mental model is obsolete.

The strategic shift: a brand can rank #1 organically and still lose the click because an AI Overview, featured snippet, or PAA panel answered the query above the first blue link. Conversely, a brand that doesn't rank in the top 5 organically can capture more clicks than #1 by winning the featured snippet or appearing in the AI Overview citation card. The work is to design content and structured data so the brand earns the features for its priority queries, not just the rankings.

For Field & Sun, this matters because the customer journey for ingredient-led skincare runs through dozens of feature-rich SERPs. "What is vitamin C serum" triggers a featured snippet + PAA + AI Overview. "Best mineral sunscreen" triggers a shopping pack + AI Overview + image pack. "Does mineral SPF leave a white cast" triggers an AI Overview + PAA + image pack. The brand that earns presence in these features intercepts demand earlier than ranking-only brands.

## Why it matters

Three reasons. First, **SERP features capture clicks that would otherwise go to organic results**. A featured snippet on an informational query routinely gets 25-40% of the click share for that query. An AI Overview citation gets brand-mention visibility plus (often) a citation link. The brand winning the feature wins the click that brand-rank-3-organic would have captured.

Second, **feature presence and AI Overview eligibility overlap heavily**. Pages that win featured snippets are disproportionately the same pages cited in AI Overviews. The content patterns are largely the same: definitive opening, fact-extractable structure, schema, freshness. Investment compounds across both.

Third, **feature opportunities are unevenly distributed across the keyword set**. Some queries trigger features that are reachable for the brand (PAA, image pack, video pack); others trigger features dominated by entities the brand can't displace (Wikipedia for the knowledge panel; large publishers for the featured snippet). Honest audit of which features are reachable beats blind feature-chasing.

## Core concepts

- **Feature inventory by query.** For each priority query, audit which features appear: featured snippet (paragraph / list / table); People Also Ask; AI Overview; image pack; video carousel; shopping pack; knowledge panel; local pack; site links; FAQ rich result. Features vary by query intent and SERP layout.
- **Featured snippet types.** Paragraph (most common), list (ordered or bullet), table, video. Different content shapes win different types. A "what is X" query usually wins a paragraph snippet; a "how to X" query often wins a list snippet; a "X vs Y" query may win a table snippet.
- **People Also Ask (PAA).** Expandable Q&A box on the SERP. Appears on most informational queries. The questions inside PAA are themselves a content-research signal — they tell you what users ask next. Earning PAA inclusion is structurally similar to featured-snippet logic.
- **AI Overview.** Google's generative answer at the top of the SERP. Cites 3-5 sources per Overview; as of 2026 present on roughly a quarter of US searches (about half of informational queries). Citation territory increasingly determined by the same authority + structure signals that win featured snippets, plus freshness, plus entity-graph alignment. AI Overviews now flow directly into **Google AI Mode**, the conversational follow-up surface — winning the Overview citation increasingly carries into the AI Mode answer.
- **Image pack.** Image carousel on visual queries (product, makeup look, ingredient demonstration). Earned by image SEO (alt text, file naming, surrounding context, schema), image quality, and original photography. Original / branded photography wins over stock.
- **Video carousel.** Video results, predominantly YouTube. For Field & Sun, the win is usually owned-channel video on key queries (own-product demonstration, white-cast comparison) — not chasing third-party video.
- **Knowledge panel.** Brand or entity panel on the right side of branded SERPs. Earned by entity recognition (Wikidata, Wikipedia, schema `sameAs`, consistent entity descriptions across the web). Slow to earn, durable when present.
- **Shopping pack.** Product images / prices for commercial queries. Earned via Google Merchant Center feed, structured `Product` schema, and product reviews. Out of scope for purely informational pages.
- **Schema as feature-eligibility signal.** Different features have different schema dependencies. `FAQPage` for FAQ rich results (legitimate FAQs only); `HowTo` for how-to rich results; `Product` for shopping; `VideoObject` for video. Schema is necessary but not sufficient — content quality is the gate.
- **Feature stability.** Some features stabilise (the featured snippet for a query stays the same source for months); others churn (AI Overview cited sources rotate weekly). Investment vs maintenance trade-off varies by feature.
- **Feature loss.** Sometimes Google removes a feature that was previously present. AI Overview rollback on certain queries; featured snippet drops; image pack disappearance. Track presence over time, not just at snapshot.

> **Scenario:** Field & Sun audits feature opportunities across the Sun Care cluster — 12 priority queries.

> - **"tinted mineral sunscreen"**: AI Overview present (Field & Sun not cited); shopping pack (Field & Sun not present); image pack (Field & Sun not present); featured snippet absent.
> - **"does mineral sunscreen leave a white cast"**: AI Overview present (cites Wirecutter, Strategist, ILIA); PAA present (4 questions); featured snippet (paragraph — currently held by an unrelated blog).
> - **"how to apply mineral sunscreen"**: featured snippet (list — currently held by Healthline); PAA present; video carousel (predominantly YouTube influencers).
> - **"mineral vs chemical sunscreen"**: AI Overview present; featured snippet (paragraph — currently held by a dermatology publisher); PAA dense (8 questions).
> - **"best mineral sunscreen for oily skin"**: AI Overview present; shopping pack; image pack; featured snippet (list).
> - **(7 more queries with similar feature mix)**
>
> **Step 2.** Reachable-feature analysis:
>
> - **AI Overview citation:** reachable on 3 of 12 (where brand has defensible angle per generative-SEO strategy); skip on the rest (publisher territory).
> - **Featured snippet:** reachable on 5 of 12 (where the current snippet source is weak / generic; brand can produce better paragraph or list with citation).
> - **PAA:** reachable on 9 of 12 (PAA inclusion is comparatively easier; structured Q&A content matching the actual questions wins).
> - **Image pack:** reachable on 4 of 12 (where original product or customer photography is differentiable; not reachable on generic ingredient explainer queries where stock medical imagery dominates).
> - **Shopping pack:** reachable on 6 of 12 with proper Merchant Center setup (Technical SEO leaf 04 + Schema); currently not earning.
> - **Knowledge panel:** not reachable in this engagement window (requires Wikidata + entity-graph work; cross-reference NLP Optimization).
> - **Video carousel:** out of scope (no owned video channel).
>
> **Step 3.** Per-feature-per-query plan:
>
> - **PAA inclusions on 9 priority queries:** add structured Q&A sections to existing pages, with answers matching actual PAA phrasing. Effort: 4 hours per page × 5 pages.
> - **Featured snippet captures on 5 priority queries:** rewrite the question-led H2 + answer paragraph or list block. Effort: 1-2 hours per page.
> - **AI Overview citations on 3 priority queries:** out of scope here; covered by `design-citation-magnet-content` per generative-SEO strategy.
> - **Image pack inclusions on 4 priority queries:** original product photography on the priority pages with descriptive alt text, file naming, schema `ImageObject`. Effort: 2 hours photography + 1 hour per page.
> - **Shopping pack inclusions on 6 priority queries:** verify Merchant Center setup; ensure `Product` schema is implemented per PDP; ensure product feed has proper attributes. Effort: cross-reference with Technical SEO Schema audit.
>
> **Step 4.** Implementation prioritised by traffic-weighted impact + effort:
>
> - **Week 1:** featured-snippet captures on top 3 traffic queries (mineral-vs-chemical, how-to-apply, white-cast).
> - **Week 2-3:** PAA structured Q&A across top 5 pages.
> - **Week 4-5:** original product photography session + image pack treatment on 4 pages.
> - **Week 6:** Merchant Center / `Product` schema audit + remediation.
> - **Ongoing:** AI Overview work runs on the citation-magnet brief track separately.
>
> **Step 5.** Result at 60 days: featured snippet captured on 3 of 5 targeted queries; PAA inclusions on 7 of 9; image pack appearance on 2 of 4. Estimated cumulative SERP-feature-driven traffic lift: +15-25%.

## How to do it

1. **Audit feature presence per priority query.** Run `audit-serp-feature-opportunities` to map current state per query and identify reachable features.
2. **Be honest about reachability.** Some features are dominated by entities you can't displace (Wikipedia for knowledge panels on generic terms; established publishers for featured snippets on commercial queries). Don't plan against unreachable features.
3. **Prioritise by traffic × reachability.** Top-traffic priority queries × reachable features = the highest-leverage work.
4. **Featured snippet captures.** For reachable queries: rewrite the page section (often an H2 + answer paragraph or list) to match the snippet pattern. Featured-snippet content tends to be 40-60 words for paragraph snippets; 6-10 items for list snippets.
5. **PAA inclusions.** Add structured Q&A sections to pages with answers matching actual PAA phrasing. Don't manufacture FAQ schema with fake questions.
6. **AI Overview citations.** Cross-reference with `plan-generative-seo-strategy` — AI Overview work is strategic and is briefed via `design-citation-magnet-content`.
7. **Image pack treatment.** Original product / customer photography; descriptive alt text; file naming; `ImageObject` schema; surrounding context that explains the image's relevance to the query.
8. **Shopping pack.** Verify Merchant Center setup; `Product` schema on PDPs; review-rich product pages; coordinate with Technical SEO leaf 04 (Schema Markup) and the broader e-commerce SEO surface.
9. **Knowledge panel.** Slow-burn entity work — Wikidata, Wikipedia where notable, `Organization` schema with `sameAs`, consistent entity descriptions across the web. Cross-reference NLP Optimization.
10. **Track feature presence over time.** Use rank-tracking tools that capture feature presence (Ahrefs, Semrush, STAT). Feature drops are early signals of broader content / authority issues.

## Common pitfalls

- **Chasing every feature on every query.** Some features aren't reachable for D2C brands without major investment. Audit reachability honestly; concentrate effort.
- **Featured snippet rewrites that lose ranking.** Restructuring a page to win the snippet sometimes hurts the page's broader ranking. Test on lower-traffic pages first; track ranking + feature presence post-rewrite.
- **PAA gaming with manufactured FAQs.** `FAQPage` schema with fake questions designed to match PAA phrasing is schema theatre. AI systems and humans both detect.
- **Stock photography for image pack.** Stock medical / beauty imagery loses to original product / customer photography. The work is to produce original visuals, not to optimise stock metadata.
- **Knowledge panel chasing without notability.** Brands without notability cannot win Wikipedia entries; aggressive Wikidata submissions can damage standing. Slow-burn entity work, not feature-hunting.
- **Ignoring feature loss.** A featured snippet present for 6 months that suddenly drops is a signal — content went stale, competitor produced better content, or Google's quality bar shifted. Investigate, don't ignore.
- **Treating AI Overview as a separate game.** AI Overview citation behaviour overlaps heavily with featured-snippet eligibility. Run them as one workstream, not two.
- **Schema as substitute for content quality.** Adding `HowTo` schema to a thin step-by-step page doesn't earn the rich result. The content has to merit the feature.
- **Forgetting mobile rendering.** Featured snippets, PAA, image packs all render differently on mobile. Optimisation that works desktop-only loses share where most traffic now is.
- **No measurement.** Feature presence isn't visible in default GA4. Use rank tracker, GSC performance + appearance filters, and `audit-llm-visibility` to capture state.
