## 1. SEO Scope

- **Target site / domain:** <https://fieldandsun.com>
- **Primary market(s) for SEO:** US primary; Canada and UK secondary.
- **Primary language(s) for SEO:** English.
- **Primary SEO audience:** Skincare-literate women 28-45 searching for ingredient-led daily skincare, vitamin C serum guidance, mineral SPF, and no-white-cast sunscreen.
- **SEO-relevant business context summary:** Field & Sun is a D2C skincare brand with two hero products: Daily Glow Serum as the evergreen reorder engine and Mineral Sun Drops SPF 50 as the seasonal sun-care hero. SEO should support high-intent product discovery and authority-building education around ingredient specificity, mineral SPF wearability, and refillable routines.
- **Entity relationships that affect SEO:** None.

## 2. SEO Goals and Priorities

- **Primary SEO goal for the next 6 months:** 2x organic sessions to roughly 17,000 per month, with organic search delivering 30% of new-customer acquisition (currently around 18%).
- **Secondary SEO goals:** Rank top 3 for the "tinted mineral sunscreen" cluster before the next Q2 peak season.
- **Priority products, services, or topics:** Mineral Sun Drops SPF 50, Daily Glow Serum, tinted mineral sunscreen, no-white-cast SPF, vitamin C serum for dark spots, refillable serum, niacinamide + hyaluronic acid serum, mineral vs chemical sunscreen.
- **Commercial outcomes SEO should support:** New customer acquisition, Serum repeat-purchase flywheel, Sun Drops seasonal demand capture, and Routine Bundle AOV lift.
- **Constraints:** 3-person team; content production budget around $2k/month; no dedicated dev resources beyond Shopify theme tweaks; Q2/Q3 demand spike creates inventory and content pressure simultaneously.

## 6. Topic, Keyword, and Page Priorities

- **Seed topic areas:** Mineral sunscreen, tinted mineral sunscreen, no-white-cast SPF, vitamin C serum, niacinamide, hyaluronic acid, refillable skincare, daily skincare routine.
- **High-intent keyword themes:** tinted mineral sunscreen, mineral SPF no white cast, vitamin C serum dark spots, refillable serum, SPF lip balm, mineral sunscreen for dark skin.
- **Comparison / alternative themes:** mineral vs chemical sunscreen, best mineral sunscreen, best vitamin C serum, serum refill economics, Supergoop alternatives.
- **Educational / authority themes:** how mineral sunscreen works, zinc oxide in sunscreen, vitamin C oxidation, niacinamide and hyaluronic acid pairing, ingredient percentages and clinical context.
- **Priority page types:** Product detail pages, collection pages, comparison guides, educational blog posts, refill routine pages, bundle pages.
- **Existing pages to protect or improve:** Daily Glow Serum PDP, Mineral Sun Drops SPF 50 PDP, `/collections/sun-care`, `/blog/mineral-vs-chemical-sunscreen`, `/blog/vitamin-c-serum-guide`.
- **New pages to create:** Tinted mineral sunscreen cluster pages, no-white-cast SPF guide, refillable serum economics guide, seasonal sun-care content, comparison pages where legally and editorially safe.
- **Topics, claims, or keywords to avoid:** Unsupported safety claims; vague category language; "clean beauty," "non-toxic," "chemical-free," miracle claims, and medical/dermatological claims not supported by review.
